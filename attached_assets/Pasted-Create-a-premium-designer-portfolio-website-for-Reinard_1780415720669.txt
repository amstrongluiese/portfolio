Create a premium designer portfolio website for **Reinard Canero**, a creative designer specializing in digital design, branding, visual storytelling, UI/UX, graphic design, and creative direction.

## Core Design Concept

The entire website should feel like a handcrafted scrapbook, journal, mood board, and creative sketchbook combined.

Theme inspiration:

* Cut-out paper art
* Handmade scrapbook aesthetics
* Botanical and nature-inspired elements
* Soft green color palette
* Layered paper textures
* Sticky notes
* Torn paper edges
* Masking tape decorations
* Polaroid frames
* Pressed leaves
* Sketch doodles
* Notebook elements
* Organic design layouts

The overall feeling should be:

* Cozy
* Cute
* Modern
* Artistic
* Playful
* Premium
* Creative
* Interactive
* Memorable

Not childish.

Not cartoonish.

Professional while maintaining warmth and personality.
